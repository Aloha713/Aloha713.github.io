I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Ajou University.<br>
I am interested in how soft and deformable systems move, adapt, and self-organize under nonequilibrium conditions, driven by energy input, environmental changes, and interactions with their surroundings.

Since my undergraduate years, I have gained research experience through experiments, image-based analysis, and physical interpretation.<br>
I enjoy starting from subtle observations, developing the tools needed to investigate them, and connecting complex behaviors to their underlying physical mechanisms.

Rather than being defined by a single material or research topic, I aim to develop a flexible research approach that integrates experiments, quantitative analysis, and physical reasoning across different soft-matter systems.
